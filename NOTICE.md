py_babelc is a Python re-implementation of **babelc**, created by
Mattias Sjösvärd (mr@seasword.com). The behaviour, annotations, CLI and the
form of the generated C++ follow the original babelc, and parts of the
original are reused:

- `babelc_py/templates/json_parser.j2` and the copies of it in
  `tests/reference_output/*.json.h`: the embedded `babel::JSON::parser`,
  MIT licensed, copyright Mattias Sjösvärd.
- Templates under `babelc_py/templates/` reproduce the text of the C++
  generated by the original IPC and JSON generators.
- Test fixtures and reference outputs in `tests/` were produced by, or taken
  from, the original babelc.
